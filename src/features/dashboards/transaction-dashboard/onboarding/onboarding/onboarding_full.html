How to Get Your Documents
📋 Business Registration Certificate
  1. Visit eCitizen portal (business.go.ke)
  2. Create/login to your account
  3. Click "Register Business Name"
  4. Fill in business details (KES 150 fee)
  5. Wait 3-5 days for approval
  6. Download certificate
🏛️ County Business Permit
  1. Visit your county government office
  2. Bring: ID, business registration, passport photo
  3. Fill application form
  4. Pay fee (KES 5,000-15,000 depending on county)
  5. Wait 1-2 weeks for inspection & approval
🏦 Bank Statement (3 months)
  1. Login to your online banking OR visit branch
  2. Request statement for last 3 months
  3. Download as PDF
  4. Ensure it shows your name and account number
💡 Utility Bill
  1. Any recent bill: KPLC, Water, Internet
  2. Must show your name and address
  3. Must be less than 3 months old
  4. Download from provider's portal or use physical copy
