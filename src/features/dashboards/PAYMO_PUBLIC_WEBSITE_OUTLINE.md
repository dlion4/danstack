# PayMo Digital BaaS — Public Website Pages Outline (Expanded)

> **What is PayMo?** PayMo is a digital banking platform that gives businesses, developers, and individuals everything they need to send money, receive payments, pay bills, manage cards, and build payment apps — all in one place. No branch visits. No paperwork mountains. Just fast, simple money tools that work.

> **Who is it for?** Business owners who want to move money easily. Finance teams who need to track every shilling. Developers who want to build payment features into their apps. Anyone tired of slow, complicated banking.

---

## Design Principles

| What works | Source | PayMo's version |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| **One-sentence hero** | Mercury | Every page opens with one clear statement |
| **Show the product** | Stripe | Real screenshots on every feature page |
| **Numbers build trust** | Wise | "47+ banks, 98.7% success, 2.1s speed" |
| **Fee transparency** | Wise | Every cost shown before asking for money |
| **Social proof** | Mercury | Customer quotes, logos, case studies |
| **Clear next step** | Stripe | Every section ends with one button |
| **No jargon** | Wise | "Send money" not "initiate transfer" |
| **Mobile-first** | Revolut | Every page works on phone first |
| **Exception-first** | Brex | Surface problems, hide what's on track |
