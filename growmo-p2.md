| Step | Action | Details |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Gather tools | Clean hand trowel or auger, clean plastic bucket (not metal), plastic bags, marker pen |
| 2 | Divide plot into zones | If plot is uniform: 1 zone. If slope/soil changes: 2–4 zones, sample separately |
| 3 | Walk in a W or Z pattern | Across the entire zone |
| 4 | Take 15–20 sub-samples | At 0–20 cm depth (topsoil). One trowel-full per stop. |
| 5 | Mix in plastic bucket | Break clods, remove stones/roots/debris, mix thoroughly |
| 6 | Fill sample bag | Take ~500g from the mixed sample, put in labelled bag |
| 7 | Label clearly | Plot name, date, depth, crop to be grown, farmer name, phone |
| 8 | Keep cool | Don't leave in hot sun or vehicle. Deliver to lab within 48 hours |
| 9 | Avoid contamination | Don't touch soil with bare hands, don't use rusty tools, don't sample near manure piles, fence posts, or under trees |

### Section 25.5: Seedling Health Monitor
| Issue | Symptoms | Cause | Treatment | Prevention |
|-------|----------|-------|-----------|------------|
| Damping off | Seedlings collapse at base, water-soaked | Fungal (Pythium, Rhizoctonia) | Remove affected, spray copper fungicide | Sterilize soil mix, good drainage, don't overwater |
| Curling/yellowing | Leaves curl, turn yellow | Nutrient deficiency or overwatering | Reduce water, apply dilute liquid fertilizer | Balanced watering |
| Leggy seedlings | Tall, thin, weak stems | Insufficient light, too close | Increase light, reduce shade | Proper spacing, adequate light |
| Cutworm damage | Seedlings cut at base | Cutworm caterpillars | Dust ash/sand around stems, apply DDT dust | Clean nursery bed, remove debris |
| Aphids | Green insects on undersides | Aphid infestation | Soap spray or Imidacloprid | Netting, inspect regularly |
| Fungal spots | White/gray spots on leaves | Fungal infection | Remove affected leaves, spray Mancozeb | Air circulation, avoid overhead watering |
